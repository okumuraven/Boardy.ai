# kuzana.co — Notes for Reasoning About the Build

**Written 2026-07-27.** Source: the live public website at `kuzana.co`, read directly (not a shared
PDF/doc like the other two companion files). This is Kuzana's actual outward-facing marketing site -
useful specifically because it's the one source that's independently *verifiable* (public, no
internal access needed) and because it surfaces real numbers and team facts that don't appear
anywhere in the Playbook, the Discovery Report, or the Brand Guidelines PDF.

---

## 1. Positioning (confirms the Brand Guidelines PDF)

Tagline: **"Education for Kenya's Top 1% Entrepreneurs"** - matches the Brand Guidelines PDF's
"Education & Investment for Kenya's Top 1% Entrepreneurs" almost verbatim. Newsletter/pitch framing:
**"Building the YCombinator for African SMEs."**

## 2. Real track record (new - not in any other source)

- **174% average annual portfolio growth** across Batch 1 & 2.
- Batch 1's own public commitment, stated as a real stake: *"if companies didn't grow by 50% in 6
  months, we'd shut down. They grew 174% on average."*
- **15 investments in 2025.**
- **$40,000 total equity investment per company**, precisely: $20k cash (with "potential for up to
  $100k follow-on" - conditional, not guaranteed) + $20k in support credit (accounting, workshops,
  board, other items). Corrected 2026-07-27 after reading the real live application form at
  `form.kuzana.co/apply` directly - the marketing site's framing made this sound like a flat $20k +
  guaranteed $100k working-capital figure, which overstates what's actually committed.
- Named case studies (real, not illustrative): an agri-export company (2x revenue), a health-food
  company (5x revenue, retention 35% → 68%, raised Ksh3.5M), a soy company (4x revenue, raised
  Ksh20M), an animal-feed company (3x revenue, raised Ksh8M in capital).
- **Referral bonus: up to Ksh 50,000** if the referred applicant is accepted (the Playbook only
  referenced this program existing at `kuzana.co/referral`, without the amount).

**Note for future copy work**: this is the source of the real discrepancy flagged during this
session - the Brand Guidelines PDF's own "Say This" example (*"Every company has 2x'd revenue in 12
weeks"*) does not match this site's actual claim (*"double revenue within 12 months,"* 174%
**annual** growth). The product's current marketing copy (`LandingPage.jsx`, `Whitepaper.jsx`) uses
the Brand Guidelines' "12 weeks" wording specifically **because the product owner explicitly chose
to keep it that way** after being shown this discrepancy - not because it was independently verified
against kuzana.co. Don't silently "fix" this again; it's a deliberate, already-made decision.

## 3. Eligibility & program structure (confirms and sharpens the Playbook)

- Ksh400k-20m monthly revenue, 3 months to 5 years old (usually), operating in Kenya.
- "Ambition to be bigger than Bidco or Kakuzi" - real Kenyan conglomerates used as the aspirational
  benchmark (Bidco Africa specifically also appears in a "Ready to Build the Next Bidco?" CTA).
- Explicitly rules out "pedigree" founders and requires demonstrated founder-driven demand
  generation - matches the Playbook's Deal Flow section exactly.
- **Only 7 companies accepted per batch** - confirmed as a real, live number, not just a brand-voice
  example phrase.

## 4. Curriculum - real named workshops (more specific than the Playbook's "curriculum v2" notes)

CEO Mindset, Branding for Startups, Sales for Entrepreneurs, Customer Experience & Retention, Startup
Operations & Systems, HR for Startups, Public Speaking & Storytelling, Finance for Founders.

**Notable inconsistency**: several of these titles use the word **"startup"** directly (Branding for
Startups, Startup Operations & Systems, HR for Startups) - the live flagship site does not follow the
Brand Guidelines PDF's own "Never Say This: startup" rule. Read this as evidence the 2025 Brand
Guidelines are a newer, aspirational direction the main site hasn't been updated to match yet, not as
license to ignore that rule in Kuzana Connect's own copy - Connect should still hold to the newer
brand doc, but don't assume kuzana.co itself is a reliable voice reference to copy from.

## 5. Team (new names, not in any prior source)

- **Kyle Schutter** - CEO (already known from the Playbook)
- **Farhan Qureshi** - co-Director (not mentioned anywhere else - the Playbook's team section only
  covered Kyle, Carol/Carolyne, Felicity, and "CG")
- **Felicity Sangona** - Executive Assistant (full name confirmed; the Playbook only ever referred to
  her as "Felicity," title "Applications/Partners" owner)

Notably, **Carol/Carolyne does not appear anywhere on the public team page**, despite being
extensively described in the Playbook as a senior operator running workshops, community, and
LinkedIn. Could mean she's not customer-facing, a contractor, or the site's team section is simply
stale - not something to assume either way without asking.

Founders' real backgrounds, stated publicly: previously ran a biogas business, a Thai restaurant, an
outsourced CFO agency, a microfinance institution, tech platforms, and a farming business - confirms
Kyle's own biogas-company anecdote and Carol's MFI background from the Playbook are real, not just
illustrative color.

## 6. Investment philosophy influences (new)

Stated explicitly on the site: Warren Buffett (long-term holds, no forced exits), Montessori/Waldorf
education (hands-on, active learning - matches the Playbook's own emphasis), Africa Eats (Kuzana's
own model for a fund eventually going public, rather than exiting individual investments), a16z
(founder-friendly value-add investing), and Y Combinator - *"ambitious people bloom like dying plants
given water."*

## 7. Real brand voice examples (confirms the Brand Guidelines PDF is grounded in real copy)

Live lines, not hypothetical: *"Compliance with KRA so you don't go to jail,"* *"relax in Diani beach
and staff don't call you with their problems"* (the literal source of the Brand Guidelines' "Relax in
Diani while the business runs itself" example), *"the cheetah that chases 2 gazelles catches none,"*
a **"Chama for high performers"** framing (a real, culturally specific Kenyan savings-group
reference - good evidence of what "Kenyan rhythm" actually looks like in practice, not just a stated
rule).

## 8. "Bizi" is never used publicly

Confirmed by reading the site directly: it uses "companies," "founders," "entrepreneurs," and "SMEs"
throughout, never "Bizi." Combined with the Brand Guidelines PDF also never using it, this confirms
"Bizi" is purely internal/community-insider vocabulary (Playbook + how the community actually talks
about itself per the Discovery Report), not something used in outward marketing. Relevant if Connect
ever considers adopting the term in its own UI copy - it would be an authentic-community-language
choice, not something modeled on existing public Kuzana materials.

## 9. The real live application form (`form.kuzana.co/apply`, read 2026-07-27)

This is the actual, current screening-stage form - the button literally says "Check Eligibility,"
not "Submit Application," confirming it's the lightweight first gate the Playbook described, just
more developed than "just get an email."

**Live program facts shown on the form itself** (more current than the Playbook or the main site):
- **Batch 4 starts July 10, 2026**, soft deadline **May 31, 2026** (late applicants pushed to
  Batch 5, starting January 2027).
- Investment: **$40k total** - $20k cash (potential for up to $100k follow-on, not guaranteed) + $20k
  in support credit (accounting, workshops, board, other items). See the correction to §2 above.
- Program goal stated on the form itself: **"2x your business in 6 months with half the stress"** - a
  *third*, different timeframe from the Brand Guidelines PDF ("12 weeks") and the main site's
  Batch 1&2 framing (annual, 174%/year). Kuzana's own materials genuinely disagree with each other
  on this number across three real sources - not something this project introduced.
- **"Honesty is more important than completeness. If you don't know an answer for sure, just say
  so"** - a real, on-brand instruction worth echoing in Connect's own copy wherever it asks for
  self-reported data.
- **Only the operating CEO may apply** - the form explicitly rejects transaction advisors,
  non-operational investors, CFOs, and Executive Assistants. Advisors are directed to the referral
  program instead.

**Verbatim fields, in order**: Name you like to be called; Other names; Email; WhatsApp (not
"phone" - specifically WhatsApp); Company name; "What does your business do?" (70-140 character
limit); Track(s) applying for (Hospitality & Tourism / Logistics, Import & Export / Fintech /
Construction / Agri-Processing & Manufacturing / Retail & D2C / Cosmetics & Fashion / Other); How
did you hear about us (Referral / Facebook / Instagram / Reapplication / Kuzana staff
called-or-emailed you / LinkedIn / Google-Search / TikTok / Other); if referral, who referred you;
an eligibility checklist requiring **all** of the following checked: operating in Kenya, Ksh100m+/
month ambition within **7 years** (the Playbook's Deal Flow section says 5 years - the live form is
the more current number), agree to a professional accounting system (Zoho-style), agree to
reconcile books by the 5th of each month, founders spend 80%+ of their time and 48+ hours/week on
the business, use a reliable email server (Google Workspace-style), commit to living in Kenya ("no
telephone entrepreneurs"), want equity specifically (Kuzana does not offer loans or grants), Ksh400k+
monthly revenue traction, and self-declared "coachable - growth mindset"; a final open "any question
for us" field.

**Disclosed in the footer**: equity taken is historically **7-33%**; submitting means consenting to
be contacted by email, phone, and WhatsApp about application status; "Reapplication" being a listed
lead-source option confirms Kuzana's real process already expects and accepts repeat applicants, not
treating it as an edge case.

**Note the "track" list is genuinely different from Connect's own `industry` enum** (Agribusiness,
Logistics, Finance, Sustainability, Branding & Marketing, Consulting, Accounting, Real Estate,
Technology, Investment, Retail & Consumer Goods, Hospitality, Other) - they overlap but aren't a 1:1
mapping (Fintech vs. Finance, Construction and Cosmetics & Fashion don't exist in Connect's list at
all). See `bizi_flow.md` for how the in-app Bizi application handles this without forcing a false
equivalence between the two.
