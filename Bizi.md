# What the word means and why it exists
"Bizi" is Kuzana's own coined term (borrowed from Luni Libes, founder of Fledge Accelerator and Africa Eats) for a founder/business that's been accepted into the accelerator program. It's deliberately not "investee," "portfolio company," "subsidiary," or "startup" — the Playbook is explicit that the relationship is long-term partnership, not pump-and-flip. Kuzana's terminology rules go further: no NGO-flavored words either (incubator, program, innovation, empower, impact are all avoided) and no acronyms at all — "we don't have SOPs, we have a Playbook, so it's accessible to more people."

Important boundary I confirmed today: "Bizi" appears nowhere on the live public kuzana.co website (which uses "companies," "founders," "entrepreneurs," "SMEs" throughout) and nowhere in the Brand Guidelines PDF either. It's purely internal/community-insider vocabulary — used in the Playbook and (per the Discovery Report) in how the community actually talks about itself day to day, but never in outward marketing copy.

## How you become a Bizi
Eligibility (confirmed both in the Playbook and live on kuzana.co, worded almost identically):

* Ksh400k–20m monthly revenue
* 3 months to 5 years old, usually
* Operating in Kenya
* "Ambition to be bigger than Bidco or Kakuzi" — real Kenyan conglomerates used as the aspirational benchmark
* Explicitly rules out "pedigree" founders (ex-Google, Harvard) commanding inflated valuations — the deal has to be reasonable (1-2x revenue at most)
* Must show "demand ownership" — a real, founder-driven, repeatable customer-acquisition process, not just a good idea
* Only 7 companies accepted per batch (an actual, real number confirmed on the live site — not just brand-guide flavor text). Sourcing happens via founder decks, funder referrals, scanning supermarket shelves, agriculture/manufacturing associations, and "Tea Meetings" — informal coffee-house recruiting sessions, with a personal day-before phone call to make sure people actually show up.

The pipeline itself: Screening App → Full App → Screening Call → Document Request (mgmt accounts, CR12, bank-verified revenue) → DD Visit (on-site, 5 customer + 5 creditor references) → Reference Check → Finance/Tax Check → Intro to 3+ Sector Experts → Board Approval → Verbal Offer → Written Offer → Legal Docs + Lawyer Q&A → Share Transfer → Accounting Fix (must hit 90% compliant before funds release) → 1st tranche → Program Starts → 2nd tranche (conditional on real milestones: proper email/accounting systems, Strategy Board formed, sales/marketing strategy, dataroom, tax compliance).

## What a Bizi actually gets
* $20,000 equity investment per company (confirmed on the live site — real dollar figure, not Ksh)
* $100,000 in working-capital access
* A structured, active-learning 12-month program — real named workshops confirmed live: CEO Mindset, Branding for Startups, Sales for Entrepreneurs, Customer Experience & Retention, Startup Operations & Systems, HR for Startups, Public Speaking & Storytelling, Finance for Founders
* A personal Strategy Board — Kyle personally chairs, matches each Bizi to a Strategy Advisor, monthly recurring meeting with a strict agenda (intros, KPI review, 20 minutes of open advisor Q&A, next-month plan)
* A Bizi Buddy System — deliberately paired with a Bizi from a different company in the same batch for weekly check-ins, workshop attendance accountability, and mandatory consultation before major decisions, with real confidentiality rules
* Twice-weekly (Mon/Thu) accounting support meetings with a finance controller and Zoho implementer, run by Kuzana first, then handed to the founder to run, then delegated to their own bookkeeper — a deliberate "teach them to drive" progression
* Kuzana gets view-only reporting access, not control

## What's expected in return
* Compliance follow-ups tracked by Kyle + Felicity specifically
* Late/absent from a workshop: $50/hour or $400/full-day, deducted straight from their own Growth Budget allocation
* Each company is given exactly "one thing" to focus on at a time — Kuzana deliberately won't discuss anything else until that's solved
* Real coaching philosophy: 4 positives before any criticism, but also candid enough that Kyle's own public "reasons a company should not work with Kuzana" disclosure includes "I've been known to give direct, hopefully constructive, criticism"
* Anonymous end-of-program feedback, gated until 7 responses come in so no rating can be traced to one person

## How Bizi are organized day to day
A single Bizi typically sits in 5+ separate WhatsApp groups simultaneously: All-Bizi, their batch group, their company group, their company's Strategy Board group, their company's Accounting group, and their company's Marketing group — a real, deliberate hierarchy, not one flat community.

## The real track record (new today, from the live site)
* 174% average annual portfolio growth across Batch 1 & 2
* Batch 1's own public commitment: "if companies didn't grow by 50% in 6 months, we'd shut down. They grew 174% on average."
* 15 investments in 2025
* Real named case studies: an agri-export company (2x revenue), a health-food company (5x revenue, retention 35%→68%, raised Ksh3.5M), a soy company (4x revenue, raised Ksh20M), an animal-feed company (3x revenue, raised Ksh8M in capital)
* Referral bonus: up to Ksh 50,000 if the referred applicant is accepted
